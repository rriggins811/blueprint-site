## How the Hour Actually Goes

This is a working session, not a lecture.

**Minutes 1 to 10, situation review.** I confirm the key details from your intake form, tell you what I noticed, and we agree on what matters most today.

**Minutes 10 to 45, the actual problems.** We work through your top 3 questions. Direct and specific, not theory. Do this, then this, here is why. If you have documents, we screen share and read them together.

**Minutes 45 to 55, your plan.** I build your 30-day plan with you: the specific steps, in order, with dates.

**Minutes 55 to 60, next steps.** We confirm the plan and pick which modules you should be in next.

**RYAN'S REAL TALK:** I will be honest with you on the call. If your plan is solid I will tell you to go execute it. If something is wrong I will tell you that too. My job is to give you the truth about your situation, the same way I would want someone to give it to my family.

## The Complete Loops System

A transition does not end at move-in day. Use this follow-up framework to stay on track over the next year.

**30-Day Check-In:** Routine established? Safety concerns? Social connections forming?

**60-Day Check-In:** Senior settling in? Care level appropriate? Adjustments needed?

**90-Day Check-In:** Overall satisfaction? Right community? What is working?

**180-Day Check-In:** Long-term adjustment. Health trajectory. Financial sustainability.

**365-Day Check-In:** Full-year review. What would you do differently?

Put these on your calendar now and set the reminders. The families who follow up are the ones who catch problems early.
