If you’re reading this module, you’ve done something remarkable. You’ve decluttered a lifetime of possessions. Sorted and rightsized with care. Prepared the home. Secured the legal and financial foundation. Chosen a community. Planned and executed a move. That is months of work, emotional labor, and family coordination.

## The Final Walkthrough

Complete this 24-48 hours before closing. Use Tool 11B to check every item.

## Every Room

•  All personal belongings removed

•  Walls free of nails, holes patched

•  Light fixtures and ceiling fans working

•  Windows clean and operational

•  Floors swept and mopped

## Kitchen

•  All appliances clean and working

•  Cabinets and drawers empty

•  Refrigerator defrosted and cleaned

•  Sink and counters clean

## Bathrooms

•  All personal items removed

•  Toilets, sinks, tubs clean

•  Medicine cabinets empty

## Garage / Basement / Attic

•  Completely empty

•  Floors swept

•  No items left behind

## Exterior

•  Yard mowed and edged

•  Walkways clear

•  Trash cans removed

•  Mailbox empty

__Take photos of every room after the walkthrough. This protects you if the buyer claims something was left behind or damaged.__

## Handling Last-Minute Issues

## Buyer Requests Additional Repairs

•  Review the purchase agreement — what are you obligated to fix?

•  Get your own contractor’s quote — don’t accept the buyer’s number at face value

•  Negotiate: offer a closing credit instead of making repairs

•  Consult your agent before agreeing to anything

## Inspection Reveals Unexpected Issues

•  Don’t panic — most issues are negotiable

•  Get your own assessment of severity and cost

•  Consider a price reduction instead of repairs

•  Remember: the buyer wants to close too

## Closing Date Needs to Change

•  Communicate immediately with all parties

•  Understand the financial impact (carrying costs, storage)

•  Get the change in writing

•  Adjust your move timeline accordingly

## Closing Day

## What to Bring

•  Photo ID (driver’s license or passport)

•  Proof of homeowners insurance cancellation

•  Final utility bills showing accounts closed

•  All house keys, garage openers, access codes

•  Appliance manuals and warranties

•  Checkbook for last-minute adjustments

## What to Expect

Closing typically takes 1-2 hours. You’ll sign numerous documents. Bring reading glasses. Funds are usually wired to your account same day or next business day. Ask the closing attorney when exactly to expect funds.

## The Emotional Side

Selling the family home is a milestone. It’s normal to feel relief, sadness, anxiety, and gratitude all at once. Ways to honor the transition:

•  Take final photos of each room before the walkthrough

•  Write down favorite memories

•  Share stories with family

•  Focus on what’s ahead: the new chapter, the new community, the new beginning
