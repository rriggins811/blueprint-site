## Moving Day Doesn’t Have to Be Chaos

Moving day is where all your planning either pays off or falls apart. I’ve seen both. The families who have a plan execute a calm, organized move in 6-8 hours. The families who wing it spend 12 hours in chaos, forget essential items, and end the day in tears.

This module turns you into a project manager. The same discipline I used to coordinate construction crews, deliveries, and inspections on house flips applies directly to move management. Different tools, same principles: timeline, checklist, delegation, and a contingency plan.

__RYAN'S REAL TALK: __*The single most important piece of advice I give every family: pack the Move Day Essentials Box first, load it in your car last, and do not let it go on the moving truck. This box contains medications, documents, glasses, phone chargers, and comfort items. It’s your lifeline for the first 24 hours. If the moving truck gets delayed, lost, or the new home isn’t ready, this box keeps everyone safe and functional.*

## The 4-Week Move Timeline

## 4 Weeks Before

•  Book movers or senior move manager

•  Confirm move-in date with new community

•  Schedule utility disconnections and connections

•  Order packing supplies

•  Start packing non-essential items

## 3 Weeks Before

•  Continue packing non-essentials (seasonal items, books, extra linens)

•  Label ALL boxes: room \+ contents

•  Submit change of address with USPS (do this online at usps.com)

•  Schedule post-move cleaning for old home

## 2 Weeks Before

•  Pack majority of belongings — leave only daily essentials out

•  Confirm move date and arrival time with movers

•  Transfer prescriptions to pharmacy near new home

•  Arrange pet care for move day

## 1 Week Before

•  Pack everything except Move Day Essentials Box and First Night Box

•  Defrost and clean refrigerator

•  Final walkthrough of new home to verify readiness

•  Label rooms in new home with signs so movers know where boxes go

•  Confirm movers one final time

## Professional Movers vs. Senior Move Manager

__Professional Movers ($1,500-$5,000): __Load, transport, and unload. You handle packing, unpacking, and coordination. Best when you have local family to manage the process.

__Senior Move Manager ($3,000-$10,000\+): __Full-service: packing, hiring movers, unpacking, setting up the new home, disposing of unwanted items. Best for out-of-state families, complex moves, or when you want completely hands-off.

__A senior move manager is the most underrated investment in the transition. They’re project managers who specialize in reducing stress. The new home is set up — bed made, pictures hung, coffee maker ready — before the senior walks in.__

__RYAN'S REAL TALK: __*If your family lives out of state, hire a senior move manager. It’s not optional. Trying to coordinate a move remotely with a moving company, utility transfers, cleaners, and a stressed parent on the other end of the phone is a recipe for disaster. The $5,000-$8,000 for a move manager saves you $2,000 in plane tickets, a week of vacation time, and your sanity.*

## The Move Day Essentials Box \+ First Night Box

## Move Day Essentials Box (Goes in YOUR Car)

__Medications: __Full week’s supply, prescription list, pharmacy contact info

__Documents: __IDs, insurance cards, POAs, contact lists, new home paperwork

__Personal: __Eyeglasses, hearing aids, phone \+ charger, toiletries, change of clothes

__Comfort: __Favorite snacks, bottled water, familiar blanket

__Logistics: __Keys to both homes, cash, credit cards

## First Night Box (Goes in Your Car Too)

__Bedding: __Sheets, pillows, blankets for the first night

__Bathroom: __Towels, toilet paper, soap, toothbrush/toothpaste

__Kitchen: __Coffee maker, mugs, snacks, paper plates, basic utensils

__Comfort: __2-3 familiar photos or a favorite throw to make the space feel like home immediately

## Move Day: A Project Manager’s Approach

## The Day Before

•  Label each room in new home with signs

•  Protect floors with cardboard or plastic

•  Confirm movers’ arrival time

•  Charge all phones and devices

## Move Day

•  Designate ONE person to direct movers at each location

•  Final walkthrough of old home — check every closet, cabinet, and storage area

•  Take photos of any pre-existing damage to furniture

•  Move Day Essentials Box and First Night Box go in YOUR car, not the truck

## First 24 Hours at New Home

__Priority 1 — Bedroom: __Make the bed with fresh linens. A place to rest.

__Priority 2 — Bathroom: __Unpack toiletries, towels, medications. Safety first.

__Priority 3 — Kitchen: __Coffee maker, mugs, snacks, basic utensils.

__Priority 4 — Comfort: __Hang a few photos, place the familiar blanket. Home.

__Do NOT try to unpack everything on day one. The goal is safe, comfortable, and familiar. Everything else can wait.__
