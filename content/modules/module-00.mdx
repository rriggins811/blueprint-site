## How to Use This Blueprint

The Blueprint has 20 modules covering every aspect of the senior transition, from the first conversation to being settled in the next chapter. But you don't have to do all 20 right now. In fact, trying to do everything at once is one of the biggest mistakes families make.

### Three Ways to Work Through This

**Path 1, Linear (Recommended for most families):** Start at Module 1 and work your way through in order. Each module builds on the one before it. If you have 3-12 months, aim for 2-3 modules per month. This gives you the most comprehensive preparation.

**Path 2, Urgent (For families in crisis):** If you're dealing with a health emergency, a fall, or a safety situation that demands action in the next 0-3 months, skip straight to Module 5 (Safety & Repairs), Module 9 (Home Sale Strategy), and Module 10 (Move Coordination). Get the senior safe first. Come back to the earlier modules once the immediate crisis is handled.

**Path 3, Early Planning (For families with 1-5+ years):** Start with Modules 1-4 at a relaxed pace. Focus on gradual decluttering, financial preparation, and early family conversations. You have the gift of time, use it wisely so you never end up on Path 2.

**PRO TIP:** Module 1 will help you figure out which path is right for you. If you're not sure where to start, start there.

## Your First 7 Days, Quick Start Plan

Don't try to do everything at once. Here's your first week mapped out in small, manageable steps. Each one takes 20-30 minutes.

### Days 1-2: Figure Out Where You Are

- ☐ Complete Module 1, identify your transition stage and timeline
- ☐ Fill out the Starting Point Quick Assessment (Module 1 toolkit)
- ☐ Fill out the Timeline Reality Check (Module 1 toolkit)
- ☐ Decide which Blueprint path you're following (Linear, Urgent, or Early Planning)

### Days 3-4: Address Safety First

- ☐ Walk through the home and identify obvious safety hazards (loose rugs, poor lighting, cluttered walkways)
- ☐ Fix anything that could cause a fall TODAY, this doesn't require a module, just common sense and 30 minutes
- ☐ If the senior lives alone, download SeniorSafe (app.seniorsafeapp.com) and set up the daily I'm Okay check-in

### Days 5-6: Get the Family on the Same Page

- ☐ Send the Family Sharing Letter (Module 0 toolkit) to involved siblings and family members
- ☐ Read Module 13's overview on family communication, even if you don't do the full module yet, understanding the dynamics early prevents blowups later
- ☐ Start a shared note or group text with family members to keep everyone informed

### Day 7: Build Your Rhythm

- ☐ Schedule recurring time this week for Blueprint work, even 30 minutes twice a week adds up fast
- ☐ Begin Module 2 (Decluttering) if you're on the Linear or Early Planning path
- ☐ Begin Module 5 (Safety & Repairs) if you're on the Urgent path

**RYAN'S REAL TALK:** Seven days from now, you'll have a clear picture of where your family stands, the obvious safety risks addressed, your family looped in, and a plan for what comes next. That's more progress than most families make in six months of worrying without acting. Give yourself credit for that.
