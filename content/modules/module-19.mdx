## The Complete Loops System: Your Follow-Up Framework

A transition doesn’t end at move-in day. The Complete Loops system is a structured follow-up framework that ensures nothing falls through the cracks over the next year.

**30-Day Check-In:**Is the routine established? Any safety concerns? Social connections forming? Adjustment challenges? (Tool 12C)

**60-Day Check-In:**Is the senior settling in? Care level appropriate? Space adjustments needed?

**90-Day Check-In:**Overall satisfaction? Is this the right community? What’s working and what isn’t?

**180-Day Check-In:**Long-term adjustment. Health trajectory. Financial sustainability. Family dynamics.

**365-Day Check-In:**Full-year review. What would you do differently? What advice would you give another family?

**Schedule these check-ins on your calendar right now. Set reminders. The families who follow up are the families who catch problems early and celebrate progress.**
